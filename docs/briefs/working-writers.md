# Extra notes for the Working Writers track

- Follow brief.md and brief-addendum.md (Try it is required, credit origins, extra "glossary" key). No "scenarios".
- These are living or recent writers, so accuracy about what they actually said matters even more. Ground every
  claim in a specific, named source: a book, essay, lecture, or published interview (outlet and year). If you can't
  tie a craft claim to a source you're confident of, leave it out. Quote only lines you're certain are verbatim
  (at most one or two per lesson); paraphrase everything else and say where it comes from.
- Focus on craft they've described and that a reader can apply, not biography. "Who and when" stays brief.
- Read and cross-reference related lessons rather than repeating them (e.g. *Jon Franklin*, *Jack Hart*, *John McPhee*,
  *Tom Wolfe & the New Journalism*, *Janet Malcolm & John Hersey*, *Journalism's story types*, *Story vs. telling*,
  *George Saunders*, *Jonathan Franzen*, *Dialogue reveals character*, *Robert McKee*, *Lynn Nottage* ↔ *Stage forms*).
